Don’t Make Fun of the Robots

By Antreas Antoniou. Originally posted on LinkedIn, alongside a shared video comparing robotics demonstrations. Original wording preserved.

I remember watching the 2015 one as it was happening and thinking 'don't make fun of the robots, this is very hard work, having to program actuators in just the right way is such an intractable task for a human', seems I was right, we just needed a way to automate the programming via deep learning, and the difference is kinda insane, reminds me of the whole theory of learning as being a massive attractor field in nature, as soon as learning in lifetimes was unlocked, pretty much every organism adopted it, barring some amoebas, but those guys have so many genes, and such a short lifespan, they might as well be doing lifetime learning via evolution.
