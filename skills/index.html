The design principle

The useful part is usually the boundary.

These skills do not pretend one more prompt will solve reliability. They make the handoff visible: between independent reviewers, between a plan and the world, between private evidence and public prose, or between a secret and the smallest process allowed to use it.

The public collection

Fifteen tools. Six operating surfaces.
