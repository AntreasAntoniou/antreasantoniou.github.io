What the Fuck Is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

By Antreas Antoniou. Originally posted on LinkedIn. Original wording and paragraphs preserved.

While sitting on the steps in George Square, taking in the Fringe ambience and flirting with the idea of early dinner in the form of a glorious baked potato or bratwurst or more than likely both, it occurred to me:

“What the fuck is a Forward Deployed Engineer and why is everyone on my linked in claiming it as their title or one of their capabilities?”

That was the beginning of a rabbit hole explored through personal and agentic deep research (and subsequent generation of AI podcasts that I could listen on the go).

I am sure you heard when OpenAI and Anthropic started using it to brand their new deployment factory spinouts, but what is it really? Is it just another buzzword without much substance if you are someone who cares about systems and technical details, or is it a more precise description of something real and valuable just expressed better?

My one sentence understanding of it is:

“A strong engineer, with high ambiguity tolerance that is ODST style dropped from orbit directly from the center of mass of AI frontier and insight into a customer facing company and asked to convert their insights and skills into better systems and processes for the given company, often being the architects of systems using cutting edge and usually AI technologies that were previously akin to witchcraft that enable a whole product and service franchise”

Yes, witchcraft is too strong, but it does seem to capture what people hiring these people expect. Like prometheus bringing fire to humanity, they are expected to traverse different systems and social layers and bring frontier technology to dinosaurs. 🦖

Jokes aside, the term does a good job capturing the idea of an unusually hands on and highly technical expert that has been molded by the frontier itself better than consultant or engineering/deployment consultant, that being said it does feel a bit too militaristic for my taste.

Perhaps something along the lines of “Frontier Transfer Engineer” or “Applied Frontier Engineer” might be more apt. But if we are talking precision and maximum nerdiness “Frontier Transduction Engineer” dominates.

For more random rambles stay tuned to this profile. And yes, early dinner it is.
