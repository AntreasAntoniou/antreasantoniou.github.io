Temporal Audio Language and Images

TALI is a multimodal dataset linking Wikipedia photographs, captions and articles to clips from YouTube recordings, their sound and available timed subtitles. A single reference image can lead to two different recordings.

Watching the stored video end to end would take about 56 weeks (12.9 months). The release links 143,250 recordings to 105,544 Wikipedia image–text entries.
